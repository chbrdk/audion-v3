# Chat step strip — persona moment channels (2026-08-12)

## Feedback

After the “one compact Denken preview” change, researchers lost Gesehenes / Gefühl / Zielnähe at a glance. Product need: **per step** see what the persona sees, thinks, feels, and whether they are progressing toward the goal — not a single truncated teaser.

## Rule

- Compact: always show useful **persona moment** lanes open — Gesehenes, Denken, Nächster Schritt, Warum, Gefühl.
- Never show empty labels or bookkeeping stubs (`Start`, dumps).
- Expand: + Schon gewusst, Neu gelernt, Ergebnis, observations.
- Compact text: allow enough lines so VO is readable (not 1–2 word clamps).
